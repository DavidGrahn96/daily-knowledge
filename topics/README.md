# Topic lists

One file per area: the 110 planned topics in publication order (001–015 already published; 016–110 written in batches 1–5 of 19). Not used by the site.

Checks run before writing:

- **Near-duplicates:** TF-IDF similarity scan over all 1,100 titles and bodies plus shared distinctive title words, within and across areas. 23 cross-area overlaps were resolved by replacing one side (e.g. the Popol Vuh kept in arts, dropped from philosophy).
- **Trivia:** every title reviewed by hand against the rule "something an educated person is expected to know".
- **Spread (≤ ~50% European/North American in history, arts, music, philosophy):**

| Area | Western share of the 95 new | Western share of all 110 (001–015 estimated) |
|---|---|---|
| History | 25% | 34% |
| Art and literature | 39% | 47% |
| Music | 39% | 47% |
| Philosophy and religion | 35% | 40% |

Every block of 19 mixes eras and regions; no two consecutive topics share both era and region.
